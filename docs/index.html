EspRangeTest

map two fingers to move
Flash two boards, leave one behind, and walk away with the other plus this page.

Connect to either board — each one reports what it can hear, so the numbers are for the link between them. Tap Connect a board again to add the second one.

For a range walk, drop one board, tap set base where you left it, turn on GPS, and walk. Every packet gets pinned to where you were standing when it arrived, so bare track is ground where nothing got through.

A board with a GNSS module draws its own track, and every packet to or from it is pinned to where it was instead. Connect to any board - the one with the GNSS or one that heard it - and the track fills in from whatever each has seen; a board that was out of reach sends what it stockpiled once you reconnect.

field turns one link into a coverage map: pick the two boards, the protocol and a stretch of time when nothing but position changed, and the packets heard and missed are spread into colour between and around where they landed. Stripes mark guesses - nothing was measured near there - and trace lays what it was made from over the top. With a base set, bearing lets a walk out in one direction stand in for the others.

Needs Chrome on Android or desktop. Web Bluetooth won't work from a file:// page — use the GitHub Pages URL, or python3 -m http.server and open http://localhost:8000.
